# THE DISTAFF SIDE — Bible Index

*An erotic court setting. Main flavor Austrian/Viennese, second layer Catalan (with Castilian in the neighbor to the west). Present day of play: spring 1699 n.H.*

**The world in one breath.** The Double Crown of **Valdau and Rocalba** joins an Austrian-flavored upland kingdom (capital **Wiedenbrunn**, on the river Selde) to a Catalan-flavored coastal principality (port-capital **Llucmar**). Three Arts are real: **the Sky** (astrology, *when*), **the Work** (alchemy and metallurgy, *how*), and **the Cards** (22 Tarock Trumps and 48 Rocalban suit-cards that live in people and fall to "the nearest heart" by blood, bed and milk, *who*). Noble blood is assayed in karats like gold. The protagonist's house, **Ilsenau**, is a matriarchal Moon-and-silver house that has nursed every royal infant since 1352. Its only son slept from the age of two and woke in 1699, nineteen, with no memories.

**Writing order:** R01 → R14, then I01 → I56, then X01 → X04. Every file ends with a HOOKS section (5+ situations). Drama is not its own file; it lives in the hooks.

## Canon skeleton (locked for consistency across files)

**Time.** Years count *n.H.* (*nach der Hochzeit*; Rocalban *d.N.*, *després de les Noces*), from the year the first Witnesses came down from Montalba. Present: spring 1699.

**Crown.** King Albrecht VII of Greifenstein, reigning since 1687 (personality left to cast), also Comte-Príncep de Rocalba, represented in Llucmar by a Lloctinent. Councils: the Kronrat (Valdau), the Corts with four braços (Rocalba), the Seven Chairs (succession).

**Blood.** Karat 0–24. Commoners 0–4 · pit-blood mining families 6–14 · minor nobles 6–12 · great houses 14–21 · 22+ is "fine" and risks brittleness · the Glass King is assayed at 23.

**The protagonist (fixed facts only).** Born to Ilsenau in 1680. Fell into the Bleischlaf in 1682, aged two. Tended in the Stille Haus by Schlafwärterinnen with daily Gliederwalken. Woke in spring 1699, nineteen, able to walk, with knowledge but no memories. Body as given in the brief.

**The Quickening.** Conception to birth in 10–14 days (shorter in high-karat mothers). The child reaches full adulthood (an early-twenties body and a grown mind, learned by absorption the way sleepers learn) by the end of the mother's Sechswochen, about 42 days, and then stops growing. The growing weeks are a closed nursery. Nothing sexual exists before the standstill.

**Coin.** 1 Greif (gold) = 10 Bräutlinge (silver) = 240 Kupferlinge. Rocalba: the gold núvia ≈ 1 Greif; copper menuts.
